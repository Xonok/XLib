# stockroom — finish the module move

I have finished regrouping the package. The flat pile of modules at the top of `stockroom/`
had grown into a list nobody could navigate, so everything is now under `core/`, `config/`,
`domain/`, `reporting/` and `io/`. **The files are in their final places and the project
does not run.**

What is left is the imports. Every `from stockroom import …` that names a module which
moved has to point at its new package. `moves.json` is the list I generated from the move
and `SPEC.md` has the same table; they are the authority on what went where.

A few things I care about, because this is the second move we have done this year:

- **Do not put anything back at the old paths.** A module that re-exports from its new
	home looks harmless and hides every reference that was not updated — which is exactly
	the thing we are trying to find. There are no compatibility modules in this project,
	and there will not be one after this either.
- **Keep `stockroom.items` and `stockroom.problems` where they are.** They are imported by
	code outside this tree at those paths and they did not move.
- **Absolute imports only.** No relative imports, no `importlib`, no `__getattr__` tricks.
	The tree imports as `stockroom.<package>.<module>` and it stays that way.
- **Do not change behaviour to make the tests pass.** The totals are reconciled against the
	supplier's export. If a test fails, the import is wrong, not the test.
- **`check.py` and `moves.json` are the ground truth and are not yours to edit**; neither
	are the tests. Fix the imports, then confirm with `check.py`.

The data files under `stockroom/data/` are generated by the supplier's export — read them,
do not edit them.
